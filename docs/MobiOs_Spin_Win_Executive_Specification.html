3. The Pacing Formula (How We Prevent Morning Drain)

To prevent the marketing disaster of burning all top prizes before lunch:

Elapsed Fraction (f) = (CurrentTime - DayStart) / (DayEnd - DayStart)
Released Target = Floor(f × DailyTargetAllocation)
Available Paced Quota = Max(0, Released Target - AwardedToday)

Rule: If Available Paced Quota == 0 → Probability drops to 0.00% automatically!

Real-World Example: For 10 iPhones allocated over a 10-hour day (9 AM to 7 PM):

  • 10:00 AM (10% of day): 1 iPhone unlocked. If won at 10:15 AM, the iPhone probability drops to 0% until 11:00 AM.
  • 02:00 PM (50% of day): 5 iPhones total unlocked so far. If only 3 were won, 2 are currently ready and winnable.
  • 06:30 PM (95% of day): 9 iPhones unlocked, ensuring excitement continues right up to closing time.

5. Executive FAQ for CEO

Can an attendee or employee hack the wheel from their mobile phone?
No. The spin calculation happens strictly on the backend. The mobile screen or kiosk tablet only plays an animation after the backend has already committed the win to the database. Manipulating client-side code has zero effect on the outcome.
What if 20 attendees spin at the exact same millisecond? Can we exceed inventory?
No. We use row-level atomic locks in MySQL. If only 1 unit of a reward remains, the database will decrement it for the first spin and return 0 for the remaining 19. The system will automatically reroute the remaining 19 to the fallback slice without displaying an error.
Can we adjust win rates or add new prizes during the event without taking the system down?
Yes. Administrators can adjust baseline weights and priority ranks directly in the portal. New settings take effect on the very next spin without restarting the backend or refreshing kiosks.
